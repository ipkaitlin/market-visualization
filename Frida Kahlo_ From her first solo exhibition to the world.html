Frida Kahlo: From her first solo exhibition to the world

This visualization maps the afterlife of Kahlo’s 6 works that landed in foreign museums after her solo Julien Levy debut in New York (1938). Out of 25 works shown at her first solo exhibition at the Julien Levy Gallery in New York, 6 of them now sit in the permanent collections of internationally renowned museums around the world. This story map follows their international journeys, however, setting aside pieces (9 in total) held across museums and collections in Mexico, Kahlo’s home country. The focus is on how and why certain pieces ended up outside the place where they were produced and originated (Mexico).

Markers: ◆ exhibited · ● created or changes hands · ■ enters a museum · ○ on loan. Owner (ring and bar): ♀ woman · ♂ man · pink and blue stripes for a couple · grey for an institution · dotted for unknown. Transfers: $ sale · G gift · D donation · B bequest · E inherited via estate · T institutional transfer · R left behind. Dashed route: loan. Dotted route: undocumented. Map: scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan.

The time axis is stretched around 1936–41, when most of the travelling happened, and compressed afterward.
